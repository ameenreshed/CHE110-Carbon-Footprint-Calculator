Ways to cut your carbon footprint

Each tip matches a part of your calculator result. Start with the category that is largest for you.

Transport

Walk, cycle, use buses or metro, and carpool. Keep tyres inflated and service your vehicle.

Flights

Fly less, choose direct routes, and take trains for shorter journeys.

Electricity

Switch to LEDs and efficient appliances, set the AC to 24-26 °C, and unplug idle chargers.

Cooking gas

Use pressure cookers, cover pots, and plan meals to avoid reheating.

Food

Eat more plant-based meals, buy local and seasonal, and finish what you cook.

Waste

Carry a cloth bag, segregate wet and dry waste, compost, and recycle e-waste properly.

Key terms


Carbon footprint

Total greenhouse gas emissions caused by a person, product or activity, shown as CO₂ equivalent.

Greenhouse gases

Gases such as carbon dioxide and methane that trap heat in the atmosphere.

Emission factor

CO₂ released per unit of an activity, such as per km driven or per kWh used.

Paris Agreement

The 2015 global agreement to limit warming to well below 2 °C above pre-industrial levels.
