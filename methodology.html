Calculation Methodology & Emission Factors

Carbotrack uses standardized linear models based on IPCC (Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change) guidelines and national grid baselines.

The Core Calculation Formula

Total CO₂e (kg/year) = Σ [ Activity Metric (Units/Yr) × Specific Emission Factor (kg CO₂e/Unit) ]

Each category estimates annual volume multiplied by peer-reviewed factors. For instance, private commuting calculates annual kilometres multiplied by a fuel-burn factor, adjusted for vehicle occupancy and carpooling frequency. Household electricity is divided by the number of occupants before applying the grid factor.

Academic Model Scope

This tool is built for educational demonstration in CHE110 Environmental Studies. Calculations reflect generalized regional constants rather than audited industrial ISO 14064 standards. The goal is personal awareness and proportional priority identification — not regulatory reporting.

Interpretation guide: treat outputs as directional estimates accurate to roughly ±20%, and compare your own categories against each other rather than chasing an exact number.

Sustainability Benchmarks

The calculator classifies results against four reference points:

  • 🟢 ≤ 2.2 t — Paris-Aligned / Target Compliant
  • 🟡 2.2 – 4.5 t — Moderate Impact
  • 🟠 4.5 – 7.5 t — High Impact
  • 🔴 > 7.5 t — Very High Impact

Known Limitations

Regional grid intensity varies by state and season. Aviation radiative forcing (non-CO₂ effects at altitude) is not fully captured. Embodied carbon in durable goods (electronics, furniture) is approximated rather than itemised. Food waste modelling is frequency-based, not mass-based.
