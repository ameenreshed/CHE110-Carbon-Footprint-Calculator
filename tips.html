Reduce Your Footprint

Small, consistent changes add up. Here are the highest-impact actions, ranked by typical CO₂e savings.

Fly Less

One long-haul return flight can equal 1–2 tonnes CO₂e. Prefer trains or video calls when possible.

Switch to EV or Transit

Electric cars and public transport cut per-km emissions by 50–90% compared to petrol cars.

Clean Electricity

Rooftop solar or a green tariff can cut your home electricity footprint to near zero.

Eat More Plants

Shifting from meat-heavy to vegetarian can save ~1.6 tonnes CO₂e per year.

Efficient Heating

Lower your thermostat by 1 °C and insulate — savings of up to 10% on heating emissions.

Reduce & Recycle

Compost organics, recycle metals and plastics, and buy second-hand to cut waste emissions.
