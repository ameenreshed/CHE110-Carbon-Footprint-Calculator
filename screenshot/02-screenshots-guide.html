Screenshots — Complete Capture Checklist
Every page and feature that needs to appear in your project report, with exact capture instructions.
1How to Capture Full-Page Screenshots
Fastest method: Open the page in Chrome → press F12 → press Ctrl+Shift+P (opens command palette) → type "Capture full size screenshot" → press Enter. Chrome saves the entire page as a PNG, not just the visible viewport.
Firefox alternative: Right-click anywhere → "Take Screenshot" → "Save full page" button in top-right Safari (macOS): Enable Develop menu (Preferences → Advanced) → Develop → Show Web Inspector → Cmd+Shift+P → "Capture Screenshot"

3Authentication Flow Screenshots
  • Sign-in modal — click "Sign In" in header, capture the login form
  • Sign-up modal — click "Create Account" tab, capture the registration form
  • Account dropdown — after sign-in, click the avatar in header, capture the menu
  • Mobile drawer — resize window to phone width, click hamburger, capture the slide-out menu
  • Lock panel — while signed out, visit challenge.html — capture the "Members Only" card
4Do NOT Include
Avoid these in screenshots — they will make your submission look broken:
  • The homepage visit counter showing "0" or "—" (the RPC is broken — see Testing section).
  • The dashboard.html page (all counters show "—" — missing RPCs).
  • The leaderboard after clicking "Refresh" if you have no data — screenshot after submitting at least one score.
