Report Section 03
Calculation Formula, Emission Factors & Sample Calculation
Complete mathematical model used by the Carbotrack engine, with full factor table and two worked examples.
1Core Calculation Formula
Total CO₂e (kg/year) = Σ [ Activity Metric (units/year) × Emission Factor (kg CO₂e/unit) ]

Applied independently across six categories — Transportation, Home Energy, Food & Diet, Water, Waste, and Shopping — then summed. Each category uses peer-reviewed emission factors from DEFRA, EPA, CEA, and Poore & Nemecek (2018).

2Per-Category Implementation

Exactly as implemented in js/engine.js of the source code.

Transportation

annualPersonalKm = personalKm × commuteDays × 50 vehicleEmission = annualPersonalKm × vehicleFactor × carpoolFactor transitEmission = publicKm × 300 × 0.045 commute = vehicleEmission + transitEmission flights = (domesticFlights × 250) + (intlFlights × 1100)

Home Energy

personalMonthlyKwh = monthlyKwh / max(1, householdSize) energy = (personalMonthlyKwh × 12 × 0.820) + (AC ? 140 : 0) + (Geyser ? 90 : 0) + (EV ? 220 : 0) + cookingFuel[cookingType]

Food & Diet

food = dietBaseline[dietType] + (dairyServings × 60) if (foodWaste == 'medium') food *= 1.10 if (foodWaste == 'high') food *= 1.25

Water

dailyLitres = (showerMinutes × 9 × showersPerDay) + (washingLoads × 60) / 7 + (tapHabits == 'lavish' ? 60 : tapHabits == 'frugal' ? 20 : 30) water = dailyLitres × 365 × 0.00035

Waste

waste = wasteBags × 52 × 4 × 0.45 waste *= (1 - recyclingRate × 0.5) waste *= 0.8 if composting

Shopping

shopping = (clothingItems × 12 × 8.5) + (onlineOrders × 12 × 2.2) shopping += 65 if plasticHabits == 'heavy' shopping -= 20 if plasticHabits == 'eco' shopping = max(0, shopping)

Classification & Eco Score

ecoScore = round(max(0, min(100, 100 - totalTons × 8.5))) if (totalTons < 2.0) rating = 'Low' → cert: Climate Champion if (totalTons < 5.0) rating = 'Moderate' → cert: Global Citizen if (totalTons < 10.0) rating = 'High' → cert: High Impact else rating = 'Very High' → cert: Severe Impact

6Model Scope & Limitations
Important disclaimer: This calculator produces directional estimates accurate to roughly ±20%. It is not a compliance tool, not ISO 14064 certified, and not suitable for regulatory reporting. It is built for educational awareness and comparative analysis — which is exactly the CHE110 assignment goal.
LimitationImpact on Result
Regional grid intensity0.82 kg/kWh is a national average — varies 0.4–1.1 across Indian states
Aviation radiative forcingOnly CO₂ counted; NOₓ and contrails excluded (~2× multiplier)
Embodied carbon in durablesElectronics, furniture, appliances approximated as zero
Food wasteFrequency-based, not mass-based
FlightsFlat kg per flight, not distance-based
Diet baselinesLiterature averages, not individual supply-chain data
