The Problem We Face

Everyday Choices Leave an Invisible Trail

From commuting to charging our electronics, individual habits compound globally. Understanding where emissions originate is the first step toward mitigation.

Transportation

Fossil fuels in private cars, aviation, and daily traffic represent the single highest individual source of tailpipe CO₂ and smog particulates.

Avg petrol car ~170g CO₂/km

Home Power Grid

Over 60% of domestic power in developing nations is still fueled by thermal coal. Continuous air-conditioning and standby appliances strain the grid.

Thermal grid intensity ~0.82 kg CO₂/kWh

Food Systems

Intensive livestock production produces high amounts of methane (CH₄), while rotting organic food in municipal dumps emits potent greenhouse gases.

Dietary variance Up to 2.5× diff

Waste & Materials

Single-use plastics, unsegregated garbage, and rapid fashion consumerism require immense petroleum extraction and high energy during fabrication.

Recycling diversion Saves ~30% CO₂
