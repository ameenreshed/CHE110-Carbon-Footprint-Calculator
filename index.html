    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is a carbon footprint?

    A carbon footprint is the total greenhouse gas emissions caused directly or indirectly by an individual, organisation, event, or product, expressed as CO₂ equivalent (CO₂e).

    How is my footprint calculated?

    We multiply your activity data (kWh, km, kg waste) by published emission factors and sum the results across four categories: home energy, transportation, diet, and waste & shopping.

    Is my data stored?

    All calculations run in your browser. Your inputs are saved locally in localStorage only — nothing is sent to a server.

    What is a good carbon footprint?

    The global sustainable target is roughly 2 tonnes CO₂e per person per year. The current global average is around 4.7 tonnes.
