How to record

1

Pick a video and a ratio

Click a card's ratio button (e.g. 9:16) to open the video in record mode. Record mode hides all UI chrome and adds a 3-2-1 countdown before playback.

2

Fullscreen the browser

Press F11 on Windows or Ctrl+Cmd+F on Mac. This hides the URL bar so it doesn't appear in your recording.

3

Set up OBS

Set canvas to match your ratio: 1080×1920 for 9:16, 1920×1080 for 16:9, 1080×1080 for 1:1. Use Window Capture and fit it to screen.

4

Record and stop

Click Start Recording in OBS when the countdown starts. Wait for the video to finish on the eco.amnrd.com scene, then stop recording.

Advanced options

  • Silent mode: add &voice=0 to any URL to disable the built-in voiceover. Record silent and add your own voice in CapCut for maximum control.
  • Loop forever: add &loop=1 to repeat the video continuously. Great for capturing multiple takes on one OBS session.
  • Single scene: add &scene=results (or any scene id: intro, problem, categories, stats, benchmarks, calculator, results, certificate, challenge, leaderboard, share, cta) to record just one part.
  • Adjust pacing: add &speed=1.15 for 15% faster, or &speed=0.9 for slower. Useful when fitting a video to a music track.
  • Voiceover quality: best in Chrome or Edge. They use Google or Microsoft online voices, which sound the most natural. Firefox audio varies by OS.
