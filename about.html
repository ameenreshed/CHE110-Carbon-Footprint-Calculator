About This Project

This calculator was developed as part of a CHE 110 group project to demonstrate how everyday activities translate into measurable greenhouse gas emissions.

Our Goal

To build an accessible, privacy-friendly tool that helps anyone estimate their annual CO₂e footprint in under two minutes.

Methodology

We use published emission factors and simple arithmetic:

footprint = Σ (activity × emission factor)

All calculations run locally in your browser — no data is transmitted.

Emission Factors Used

  • Electricity: 0.82 kg CO₂e / kWh
  • LPG: 42.3 kg CO₂e / 14.2 kg cylinder
  • Natural gas: 2.0 kg CO₂e / m³
  • Petrol car: 0.192 kg CO₂e / km
  • Diesel car: 0.171 kg CO₂e / km
  • CNG car: 0.14 kg CO₂e / km
  • Electric car: 0.05 kg CO₂e / km
  • Motorbike: 0.07 kg CO₂e / km
  • Bus: 0.08 kg CO₂e / km
  • Train / Metro: 0.04 kg CO₂e / km
  • Flights: 150 kg CO₂e / hour
  • Landfill waste: 0.5 kg CO₂e / kg
