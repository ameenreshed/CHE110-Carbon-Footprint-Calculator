# Eco Carbon Footprint Calculator — Demo Video Plan

## Main Reel (30 seconds, 9:16)

**Goal:** Make the project understandable in the first 3 seconds and show a real workflow, not just abstract green visuals.

| Time | Shot | On-screen text | Recording direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Fast close-up of the Eco landing page, cursor taps Calculate | “Do you know your carbon footprint?” | Start on the real website, no intro fade longer than 0.2s |
| 3–7s | Scroll through transport and home-energy steps | “Travel • Energy • Food • Water • Waste” | Record smooth, deliberate interactions; use sample data |
| 7–12s | Choose answers and move through the multi-step form | “A few everyday choices add up” | Keep each interaction visible for 0.5–1s |
| 12–17s | Click Calculate and show the result total | “Get your estimated annual footprint” | Let the result settle; avoid faking the number |
| 17–22s | Show category breakdown chart and comparison | “See what contributes most” | Pan/zoom slightly; keep text readable |
| 22–26s | Show methodology/assumptions page | “Transparent assumptions. Practical next steps.” | Include this so the project feels credible |
| 26–30s | End card with the wide promo art and site URL | “Explore Eco · eco.amnrd.com” | Hold final card for at least 2 seconds |

### Capture setup
- Use a clean browser profile and close notifications/tabs.
- Record at 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, with browser zoom around 90–100% so key content fits.
- Use a realistic sample profile; do not imply a sample result is a measured real-world emission value.
- Capture the actual UI with OBS or a screen recorder, then crop/scale for vertical video.
- Add subtle background music at low volume; keep click sounds optional.
- Add captions because many viewers watch without sound.
- Avoid showing email addresses, user IDs, private Supabase details, or personal account data.

## 15-second cut
- 0–2s: “Your daily choices have a footprint.”
- 2–6s: Fast form sequence (transport, electricity, food).
- 6–10s: Results + emissions breakdown.
- 10–12s: Methodology page.
- 12–15s: “Measure. Understand. Reduce. eco.amnrd.com”
